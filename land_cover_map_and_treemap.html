Land cover composition

  • Hover over a landscape region to preview its land cover composition. Select a region to keep it active.
  • Click a treemap square to isolate that land cover on the map; click it again to clear the isolation.
  • Use the drop-down or the 'Previous geography' and 'Next geography' arrows to select a region or National. Use the 'Opacity' slider to adjust the transparency of the selected region overlay. Use 'Download SVG' or 'Download PNG' to export the current view to an image.

Based on the Living England 2022-23 habitat map.
